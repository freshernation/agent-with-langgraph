# The AI roles

One AI, five characters, from the ALTER framework the course is planned with:
**A**dvisor, **L**ibrarian, **T**utor, **E**ditor, and **R**oommate — here called the
outside view. And one more for Fridays.

| Role | File | When |
|---|---|---|
| **Advisor** | `advisor.md` | Day zero: your baseline interview. Week 6 Monday: choosing your problem |
| **Librarian** | `librarian.md` | You need a source the week's reading and `SOURCES.md` do not give you |
| **Tutor** | `tutor.md` | You have been stuck for twenty minutes |
| **Editor** | `editor.md` | Your tests pass and you want to know if the work is good. Every written deliverable goes through it |
| **Outside view** | `outside-view.md` | Once a week — the question at the bottom of the week's README |
| **Defend** | `defend.md` | Friday, on your milestone, before your instructor sees it |
| **Interviewer** | `interviewer.md` | Week 6: rehearsing the mock interview |

## How to use one

Open a fresh chat. Paste the whole role file as your first message, filling in the
`[SQUARE BRACKETS]`. Then talk normally. Start a new chat for each session.

## Two things that ruin this

> "Just fix it for me."

You get working code and cannot explain it on Friday. The tutor refuses; do not go
around it.

> "Write the graph for me."

The tutor asks you to draw it first. A graph you can draw is a graph you can defend;
one that arrived from a chat window is one you cannot change on Friday.

## What goes in your log

Every role ends with a `SIGNAL` block. Copy it into `logs/signal-log.md`. It is how
your instructor knows where to spend the live hour.
